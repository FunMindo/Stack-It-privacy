Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the Game and its features. The table below details exactly what is collected, why, and where it goes.

Data Type What It Is Where It Goes Required?
Anonymous Player ID A randomly generated ID with no personal information attached. Not linked to your name, email, or device identity. Unity Gaming Services servers Auto
Display Name A name selected from a safe, predefined list curated by FunMindo. No free-text entry is possible. Only used to display your name on the leaderboard. Unity Gaming Services servers Optional

✅ FunMindo does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, certain data such as device identifiers, Advertising IDs, and a Google Play Games account identifier (if you choose to sign in) may be collected automatically by third-party services integrated into the Game — see Section 4 for details.

🔒 Signing in with Google is entirely optional. Stack It! can be played fully without an account or registration of any kind — Cloud Save simply lets your progress follow you if you choose to link a Google account.

How We Use Your Information

  • To display your chosen name and score on global leaderboards (Weekly and All-Time)
  • To save and restore your in-game progress (coins, diamonds, crowns, skins, high score, player name) across reinstalls and devices when you sign in with Google
  • To serve advertisements through Google AdMob and generate revenue to keep the game free
  • To improve gameplay experience and maintain game functionality
  • To measure ad performance and optimise ad frequency
  • To comply with legal obligations under applicable Indian law

FunMindo does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as name, email, or phone number. FunMindo does not sell, rent, or trade any personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), FunMindo processes data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — By downloading and playing Stack It!, you consent to the collection of device identifiers and Advertising IDs by Google AdMob for the purpose of serving advertisements and improving app performance. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, you separately consent to your progress being synced to Unity Cloud Save, linked to your Google Play Games identifier. You may withdraw either consent at any time — by uninstalling the Game, opting out of personalised ads (see Section 5), or unlinking/requesting deletion of your Cloud Save data (see Section 9).
  • Legitimate use — Leaderboard functionality (anonymous Player ID, predefined display name, scores) and Cloud Save functionality (progress syncing) are processed for the legitimate purpose of enabling global score rankings and letting players keep their progress across devices. Basic operational data may be processed for maintaining app functionality and preventing abuse.
  • Legal obligation — We may process and retain data to the extent required to comply with applicable Indian law, including the IT Act, 2000 and the DPDPA 2023.

5

Advertising & Your Choices

Stack It! is free to play and is supported by advertisements delivered by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to show you relevant ads based on your interests.

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"
  • Reset your Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → "Reset advertising ID"

Opting out means you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised to your interests. Game functionality is not affected.

6

Data Storage & Retention

  • Local device data (game progress, coins, settings) — stored on your device via PlayerPrefs. If you have not signed in with Google, this data is lost if you uninstall the game or switch devices.
  • Cloud Save data (coins, diamonds, crowns, owned skins, selected skin, high score, player name) — stored on Unity Cloud Save servers, linked to your Google Play Games identifier, only if you have signed in. Retained for as long as the service is active or until you request deletion (see Section 9).
  • Leaderboard name & scores — stored on Unity Gaming Services servers for as long as the service is active or until you request deletion.
  • No FunMindo backend. FunMindo does not operate its own backend servers or databases for user data storage. All user-related data is handled entirely by Unity Gaming Services (including Unity Cloud Save) and Google — FunMindo has no direct access to raw user data.

Children's Privacy

Stack It! is designed for a general audience, including children, and is rated for Everyone (3+) on Google Play. We take reasonable steps to configure advertisements appropriately for such mixed-age audiences, including limiting ad personalisation where applicable, in line with Google Play Families policy.

FunMindo does not directly collect personally identifiable information — such as name, email, or phone number — from children or anyone else. The leaderboard display name feature is designed for all ages. Because all names are predefined and curated by FunMindo, no player (including young children) can enter personal or inappropriate information — the predefined name system protects all users on the leaderboard.

The optional Google Sign-In / Cloud Save feature relies on the player's existing Google Play Games account and the account-level controls Google already provides (including Family Link, where applicable) for account creation and parental consent. FunMindo does not itself collect a child's email address, password, or other account credentials as part of this feature.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any concerns about your child's data, please contact us immediately at funmindostudio@gmail.com and we will take steps to address the issue promptly.

Our advertising is configured for a general audience. We take reasonable steps to limit ad personalisation for mixed-age audiences in line with Google Play Families policy; however, as ads are served by Google AdMob, FunMindo cannot guarantee the content of every individual advertisement served.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled through Stack It!, including Cloud Save data linked to your Google account. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at funmindostudio@gmail.com or use our Data Deletion page. We will respond within 30 days in accordance with applicable Indian law (Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).
