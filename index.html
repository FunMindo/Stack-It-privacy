Privacy Policy
Stack It!

Effective Date: April 20, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 20, 2026  ·  Jurisdiction: India

📋 This Privacy Policy describes how FunMindo ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the mobile game Stack It! ("the Game"). By downloading or playing Stack It!, you agree to the terms described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the Game.
1

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the Game and its features. The table below details exactly what is collected, why, and where it goes.

Data Type What It Is Where It Goes Required?
Anonymous Player ID A randomly generated ID with no personal information attached. Not linked to your name, email, or device identity. Unity Gaming Services servers Auto
Display Name A nickname you choose yourself before entering the leaderboard. Only used to display your name on the leaderboard. Unity Gaming Services servers Optional
Game Scores Your highest scores submitted to weekly and all-time leaderboards. Unity Gaming Services servers Optional
Device Info Device model, OS version, screen size. Collected automatically by ad and analytics SDKs for performance and ad delivery. Google (AdMob / Firebase) Auto
Advertising ID A resettable advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) used to serve relevant ads. You can reset or opt out at any time in your device settings. Google AdMob servers Auto
Crash & Performance Data Anonymous crash reports and performance metrics used to fix bugs and improve stability. Firebase Crashlytics (Google) Auto

✅ We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or any other sensitive personal information.

2

How We Use Your Information

  • To display your chosen name and score on global leaderboards (Weekly and All-Time)
  • To serve advertisements through Google AdMob and generate revenue to keep the game free
  • To analyse gameplay patterns anonymously and improve game design
  • To detect, investigate, and fix crashes and technical bugs
  • To measure ad performance and optimise ad frequency
  • To comply with legal obligations under applicable Indian law

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

4

Advertising & Your Choices

Stack It! is free to play and is supported by advertisements delivered by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to show you relevant ads based on your interests.

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"
  • Reset your Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → "Reset advertising ID"

Opting out means you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised to your interests. Game functionality is not affected.

5

Data Storage & Retention

  • Local device data (game progress, coins, settings) — stored only on your device via PlayerPrefs. Deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • Leaderboard name & scores — stored on Unity Gaming Services servers for as long as the service is active or until you request deletion.
  • Advertising & analytics data — retained by Google per their own data retention policies.
  • We do not operate our own servers and do not store any user data on FunMindo infrastructure.
6

Children's Privacy

Stack It! is a casual game designed for a general audience. It is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. The leaderboard display name feature is intended for players aged 13 and above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us immediately at funmindostudio@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

Our advertising is not child-directed. AdMob is configured to serve general audience advertisements only.

7

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled through Stack It!. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We rely on industry-standard third-party services (Google, Unity) that maintain their own security practices.

Since we do not operate our own servers or databases, FunMindo does not store, process, or have access to personally identifiable information beyond what is visible on the public leaderboard.
